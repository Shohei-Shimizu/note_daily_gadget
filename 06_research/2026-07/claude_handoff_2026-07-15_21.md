## Notes for writing

- All 38 `selected_items[*].asin` values are present and match the ASIN in each Amazon URL.
- All Amazon URLs use the `daily-gadget-22` affiliate tag.
- PA-API did not consistently return review counts or ratings. Selection therefore uses the documented fallback: Amazon search relevance, strict title matching, exclusion terms, manufacturer diversity, and URL/ASIN verification.
- 2026-07-15: YubiKey 5 NFC supports more protocols than the FIDO-only models. Do not describe every key as functionally identical.
- 2026-07-16: BeeStation and the NAS kits differ in setup complexity and whether storage is included. Check the product data before describing capacity or RAID.
- 2026-07-17: The products cover AI recording, transcription, electronic notes, and translation. Do not claim that every device provides direct conversational ChatGPT access.
- 2026-07-18: VPN support is model-specific. The older compact hotel routers should be framed as portable access-point/repeater products, not as equivalent to the GL.iNet VPN models.
- 2026-07-19: Echo Show 15 is a general smart display with calendar functions; the other products are dedicated family calendar displays.
- 2026-07-20: The list mixes Windows AI PCs and a refurbished MacBook Air. Avoid claiming identical on-device AI features or software compatibility.
- 2026-07-21: IP ratings, mobile-network support, display brightness, and included accessories vary by model. Do not generalize one model's durability specification to all products.
